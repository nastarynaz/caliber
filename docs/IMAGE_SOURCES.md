## Official Chandra Asri imagery review

Official Chandra Asri facility and workforce imagery was considered first, including the corporate site, careers site, company publications, and Chemical Solutions pages. No explicit production reuse grant was found during this implementation. Chandra Asri also publishes a warning about third parties using company information and images. Therefore no official Chandra Asri photograph has been copied into the repository.

- Corporate site: https://www.chandra-asri.com/
- Careers site: https://careers.chandra-asri.com/
- Chemical Solutions page: https://chandra-asri.com/en/our-business/chemical-solutions
- Image-use warning: https://chandra-asri.com/en/news/warning-announcement-of-a-website-using-chandra-asris-information-and-images

Production replacement with official company photography requires written approval from the relevant Chandra Asri communications or brand owner. Until then, the UI labels the shipped facility image as generic petrochemical operations context and explicitly states that it is not a Chandra Asri facility.
